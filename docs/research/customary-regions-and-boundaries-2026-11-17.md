# Customary regions of Tanah Papua and how sources treat boundary places

Research note for ETNOS. Written 17 November 2026.
Method: I fetched and read each source below on this date. Quotes are short and carry their URL. Anything I could not open or confirm is marked **[UNVERIFIED]**. Quotes are in Indonesian; the English around them is my paraphrase.

## 1. Short answer

- The "seven wilayah adat" (Mamta/Tabi, Saireri, Domberai, Bomberai, Mee Pago, La Pago, Ha Anim) is a real, widely used scheme. Government, Bappenas, MRP and Dewan Adat Papua (DAP) all use it.
- It is **not one clean legal list**. The lists of regencies per region differ from source to source. The 2022 province laws do not mention wilayah adat at all.
- The scheme was assembled from earlier culture-area work (Dutch administration, Held, Gerbrands and others). DAP, Uncen anthropologists, SIL and the provincial culture office compiled it in their 2008 ethnic mapping. DAP says it fixed the seven names in 2002. I could not confirm the commonly cited link to Mansoben 1995. The 1995 book does not use the seven names (see 2.2).
- Boundary places are contested, and the contest is usually about politics (seats, capitals, funds) as much as culture. Nabire is the clearest case. Its coast is claimed for Saireri by local leaders. Its highlands are Mee Pago. Official lists put the whole regency in Mee Pago (Bappenas 2020), or in Saireri (a provincial 2008-era profile), or split it.
- Recommendation: keep administrative units as the spine. Record wilayah adat as sourced, dated overlays with "whole / part / contested" and a stated reason. Do not force one primary region. The evidence supports this strongly (section 6).

## 2. Origin and authority of the scheme

### 2.1 Where the seven names come from

- A provincial government profile (Badan Lingkungan Hidup Papua, undated PDF) gives the lineage. It says that, per DAP, Uncen anthropology, SIL and Dinas Kebudayaan in 2008, "konsep wilayah Adat atau culture area sudah dikenal ... sejak tahun 1960-an" and merged earlier concepts: "Pembagian 6 Wilayah Administrasi oleh Pemerintah Belanda, Culture Provinsi oleh G.J. Held, Wilayah Gaya Seni oleh Wingert, Rapl Linton, A. Gerbrands dan S. Koijman". https://lingkunganhidup.papua.go.id/gi/fckimage/file/animha1.pdf
  - Note: the PDF text is OCR-damaged. The names "Rapl Linton" and "Wingert" look like OCR or typing errors. **[UNVERIFIED]** who exactly is meant.
  - The same PDF's table lists **five** regions for Papua Province, with Nabire under Saereri and Mimika under "Ha Anim" (OCR-garbled, so read with care). Papua Barat is given two (Domberai, Bomberai).
- DAP's own account: a DAP figure said in 2015 that "dalam pedoman dasar Dewan Adat Papua (DAP) pasal 6 ayat 2 wilayah adat dibagi tujuh" and "Ini ditetapkan DAP pada 2002 dalam konferensi besar masyarakat adat Papua I." He opposed later changes. https://arsip.jubi.id/weyasu-sistem-pemerintahan-tak-bisa-memisahkan-wilayah-adat/
- Dutch administration: Mansoben 1995 lists the late-colonial residencies (Hollandia, Geelvink Baai, West Nieuw Guinea, Fak-Fak, Zuid Nieuw Guinea, Centraal Bergland). In that list Nabire is not named. Yapen, Waropen, Biak and Wandamen sit together under Geelvink Baai. Mimika sits under Fak-Fak. https://www.papuaerfgoed.org/sites/default/files/collectie/files/2004-01/Mansoben_1995_Sistem.pdf (my text extract of the PDF; footnote around printed p. 59).
  - This matters. The Dutch Fak-Fak grouping of Mimika is the base for the later "Mimika is Bomberai" argument. Mimika leaders cite "peta wilayah adat Belanda" (see section 4).

### 2.2 Mansoben 1995: what it does and does not say

- Full title: *Sistem Politik Tradisional di Irian Jaya*, LIPI-RUL Seri 5, Jakarta 1995. I downloaded the 422-page PDF and searched its text. It is about four leadership types (big man, king, ondoafi, mixed), not about a seven-region map.
- Searches for "Mee Pago", "Saireri", "La Pago", "Mamta", "Domberai" and "wilayah adat" returned nothing. It does use Bomberai and Teluk Cenderawasih as physical and field-site areas. Its fieldwork areas were Jayapura, Teluk Cenderawasih, Pegunungan Tengah, south coast and Kepala Burung.
- It puts the Teluk Cenderawasih peoples (Biak, Wandamen, Waropen, Yapen, Maya) in the "mixed" leadership type, citing "Mansoben, 1995:263-307" (via Jubi): https://arsip.jubi.id/dewan-adat-papua-dan-lembaga-adat-papua-di-persipangan-jalan/
- **Finding:** Mansoben is cited by media for leadership typology, and he was a speaker on the seven regions (WRI Indonesia event 2020: https://wri-indonesia.org/id/events/2020/7/lingkar-belajar-tanah-papua-catatan-20-tahun-otonomi-khusus-papua). But the 1995 book is not the source of the seven-region map. I found no primary text linking Mansoben to authorship of the scheme. Treat "Mansoben 1995 = origin" as **unsupported**. Later Mansoben writing may exist. **[UNVERIFIED]**

**Finding on "which wilayah adat each province follows":** the claim comes from press and politics, not from the laws. In April 2022 the DPR Baleg proposed naming the three provinces after regions: "Ha Anim untuk Provinsi Papua Selatan, nama Meepago untuk Provinsi Papua Tengah, dan Lapago untuk Provinsi Papua Pegunungan Tengah" (Kompas, 8 Apr 2022: https://nasional.kompas.com/copy/2022/04/08/14125751/mengenal-wilayah-adat-ha-anim-meepago-dan-lapago-yang-diusulkan-jadi-nama). The enacted names are Papua Tengah / Selatan / Pegunungan. DPR deputy chair Saan Mustopa said in July 2022 that seven provinces were "yang paling sesuai" given seven adat regions (https://nasdemdprri.id/berita/pemekaran-papua-berdasarkan-tujuh-wilayah-adat). Only six provinces exist now. The MRP Papua Tengah site says the province "kira-kira sesuai dengan wilayah adat Meepago dan Saireri" (https://mrp.papuatengahprov.go.id/informasi-umum-provinsi-papua-tengah). That wording matches Wikipedia's. Treat it as non-independent.

## 5. Scholarly and customary-body views

- **"It is culture areas, not adat areas."** Papuan anthropologist and curator Enrico Yori Kondologit: "belum ada konsep atau definisi wilayah adat di Papua ... yang ada hanyalah ... wilayah budaya atau Culture Area" and "Tidak ada konsep 7 wilayah adat, tapi yang ada itu konsep 7 wilayah budaya saja." He says adat is narrower than culture: "Adat itu kan eksklusif secara religi, pengetahuan, dan sistem politik." He ties the term "wilayah adat" to the 2001 Otsus law. https://jubi.id/tanah-papua/2023/antropolog-papua-seharusnya-tujuh-wilayah-budaya-bukan-wilayah-adat/
  - He cites Flassy (1984, 2013) and Held and Gerbrands. His "Held (1979)" date looks odd for a scholar who published in the 1940s-50s. **[UNVERIFIED]** the dates.
- **The scheme reduces diversity and was not built for rights.** I N. Suryawan, citing Flassy (1995): culture areas were a way to cope with diversity by "mereduksinya ke dalam jumlah-jumlah yang lebih kecil". He warns: "Pemisahan administrasi dalam pemekaran-pemekaran daerah tidak perlu pararel dengan pemisahan kebudayaan". He cites Timmer (2013) against treating cultures as closed units. http://repository.uksw.edu/handle/123456789/4806 (Bird's Head focus; not Nabire).
- **Dutch map reused without checking.** Paskalis Kossay (2020): the Dutch "hanya fokus pada batasan wilayah geografis" and DAP adopted it "secara bulat-bulat". He names Nabire and Kokonao (Kamoro) as groups put in Mee Pago against their cultural ties, and proposes an eighth region. (Opinion column, but by a named Papuan intellectual.) Suara Papua URL above.
- **Splitting by region risks conflict.** Uncen anthropologist Christine Sanggenafa (Tribun Papua, June 2022): provincial division on seven regions "akan berpotensi timbulkan perpecahan". https://papua.tribunnews.com/2022/06/23/ini-kata-pakar-antropologi-uncen-soal-dob-7-wilayah-adat-christine-potensi-timbulkan-perpecahan
- **Identity used strategically.** A UGM master's thesis on Papua Tengah (2025, Herry Pragus Yeuyanan) reports tensions "antara kelompok Meepago, Lapago, dan Saireri serta antara wilayah Nabire dan Mimika" and says cultural identity "digunakan secara strategis dalam perebutan posisi birokrasi dan akses ekonomi". Thesis abstract only. https://etd.repository.ugm.ac.id/penelitian/detail/265048
- **Practical harms of a forced single label.** Legislator Maria Duwitau (Jubi, 2017): in Mee Pago MRP recruitment "suku asli Mimika itu Amungme dan Kamoro. Mereka tidak memiliki keterwakilan." Mimika's Bupati (2021) said Mee Pago grouping costs Mimika seats in MRP and DPRP. URLs above.
- **Government line.** Governor Enembe (2017) recognised only five regions in Papua Province and said the boundaries were "sudah karena ada kesamaan-kesamaan". The Jokowi quote reported by DAP Nabire (2022) says culture should not be the basis of pemekaran. RIPPP says adat unity is considered but administrative units are "dasar". 
- **Not found / gaps.** I found no published statement from MRP Papua Tengah or Dewan Adat Saireri / Meepago as bodies on the Nabire question, only the DAP Nabire chair, LMA Nabire and Wate leaders. I found no KITLV, ANU or BRIN piece addressing Nabire specifically. I did not find a Mee community or Dewan Adat Meepago rebuttal. One report says Mee leaders (Ferry You, Donatus Gobay, Andreas Pekey) supported the Nabire coastal petition (nabire.net, Feb 2022); that is a single local-news claim. **[UNVERIFIED]** from a second source. The Wanggar capital decision is described in Wikipedia and local news but I did not read a decree.

## 6. Lessons from other schemes

| Scheme | What it does | Lesson |
|---|---|---|
| Native Land Digital | Overlapping polygons by design. FAQ: "When knowledge of an area is contradictory, we respect and include all Indigenous perspectives". Founder: "in reality the polygons should overlap"; the alternative would put the mapper "in the position of deciding whose territorial claims are valid". Not a legal resource. https://native-land.ca/about/faq and https://medium.com/@native-land/a-question-of-borders-79d852adfbaa | Overlap is a feature. The platform should not adjudicate. Add a "not official" statement. |
| AIATSIS Map of Indigenous Australia | Shows "general locations of larger groupings"; "borders ... purposefully represented as slightly blurred"; "Some of the information shown on the map is contested"; "not suitable for native title or other land claims". https://aiatsis.gov.au/explore/map-indigenous-australia | State plainly what the layer is and is not for. Show contested status. |
| New Zealand (Te Kāhui Māngai) | Rohe descriptions are "a record of information supplied by representative Māori organisations" and not endorsed by the Crown. Limits: these are Indigenous-led or state systems in settler countries. Papua's wilayah adat is mostly an outside or administrative classification that Papuan bodies adopted, and it has legal-political uses (MRP and DPRP seats, planning). That makes it more like an administrative overlay than a self-description, so source attribution and dates matter more.

## 7. Recommendation for ETNOS

1. **Spine = administrative hierarchy** (province, regency, district). It is unambiguous, dated and legally defined (UU 15/2022 Pasal 3 lists Nabire, Mimika and the rest). Evidence supports this. The Papua Tengah law itself and RIPPP both use administrative units as the base.
2. **Cultural regions = separate overlay records.** Each record: place, wilayah adat, extent (`whole` / `part` / `contested`), part description (for example "coast", "highlands", "six coastal peoples"), reason, source, source year, source type (law, planning doc, customary body, scholar, press), and a quote. Evidence supports this. Nabire, Mimika and Mamberamo each have sources that disagree or that split the place.
3. **No forced primary.** Allow several records per place. Show the legal or planning designation (for example Bappenas 2020: Nabire = Mee Pago) as a labelled record, not as "the" answer. Contradicts the idea of one primary: the same regency has different values in the 2008 provincial profile, RPJMN 2020-24 and DAP's own spokesman.
4. **Attach people groups where known.** For Nabire, six coastal peoples (Wate, Yerisiam, Umari, Goa, Mora, Hugure, per local sources; the lists vary) and three highland peoples (Mee, Auye, Moi). Attaching the cultural region to the people group avoids saying "Nabire is X". Evidence is local press and one op-ed, so mark as sourced claims. **[UNVERIFIED]** list of peoples against an ethnographic source.
5. **Say who is speaking.** Include a "claimed by" field. Mimika's 2021 stance was an official government and LEMASKO/LEMASA decision with seat politics stated openly. A platform should record that context, not hide it.
6. **Record motives neutrally.** Several disputes are tied to seats and capitals (MRP/DPRP seats, Papua Tengah capital). Keep a short "context" note. Do not editorialise.
7. **Disclaimer text** (adapting AIATSIS and Native Land): wilayah adat on ETNOS are reported classifications, not legal boundaries or ulayat claims, and are contested in places.
8. **Avoid inventing the origin.** Do not state "Mansoben 1995 defined the seven regions". Say the scheme draws on Dutch and Western culture-area work, was set by DAP (2002 and 2008 mapping with Uncen, SIL and the provincial culture office) and adopted by government. Cite the provincial profile and Weyasu quote.
9. **Open items before launch:** read UU 14/2022 and UU 16/2022; read Perdasus 6/2014 and any later Perdasus that defines wilayah adat by regency; check whether DAP statutes or the 2008 mapping are published; look for Mansoben's later work on the seven regions; find a primary document on the Wanggar capital site; look for Dewan Adat Meepago and MRP Papua Tengah statements.
