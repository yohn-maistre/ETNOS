Glottolog language reference points (apps/web/src/papua-atlas.json)

Source: Glottolog, languages_and_dialects_geo.csv, https://glottolog.org
Hammarström, Harald, Robert Forkel, Martin Haspelmath and Sebastian Bank. Glottolog. Leipzig: Max Planck Institute for Evolutionary Anthropology.
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes: the latitude and longitude of the 256 languages in the Tanah Papua catalogue were projected to the
site map's Web Mercator pixel space, rounded to 0.1 px, and each point was assigned to the customary reference
region of the regency outline it falls in (or the nearest outline within 20 px). A point is a reference point
for the language, not the boundary of where it is spoken.

Region outlines in the same file are the union of the simplified Badan Informasi Geospasial regency outlines in
site-map.json, grouped by the customary reference region membership in src/catalogues/tanah-papua.json
(Narasi RPJMN 2020–2024, Bappenas). They are planning regions, not customary land boundaries.
