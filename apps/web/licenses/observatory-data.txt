Observatory data: place points, the knowledge graph's size and pace, and dataset records

Place points: apps/web/public/observatory/places.json (same as src/catalogues/tanah-papua-places.json)
Derived by scripts/observatory/build-place-points.ts from the simplified Badan Informasi Geospasial
(BIG) outlines in apps/web/public/geo/ (see map-data.txt): one reference point and one area per
catalogue place, rounded to 0.01 degree. Customary-region membership follows Narasi RPJMN 2020-2024
(Bappenas). The points mark administrative or planning areas, not particular sites, and are not
for delineating boundaries.

Language points on the observatory map and on language pages: Glottolog 5.4 reference points,
CC BY 4.0 (see glottolog-data.txt). They are reference points, not boundaries of where a language
is spoken.

Size, pace and level in apps/web/public/observatory/knowledge/graph.json: computed by the rules in
src/world-model.ts (ETNOS, AGPL-3.0). Level classes follow the main classes of the Integrative
Levels Classification (ILC2, by Claudio Gnoli and colleagues); only the class letters are used,
no ILC text, because the ILC licence is not stated.

names its Wikidata item. Danau Tage has no coordinate there yet and stays in the tray.
