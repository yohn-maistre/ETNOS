Story and place maps — basemaps, outlines and library

Library: maplibre-gl 5.24.0, BSD-3-Clause, https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre-gl-js
Loaded only when a map scrolls into view.

Basemap imagery (loaded from EOX at view time, not stored here):
  Terrain: Terrain Light © EOX IT Services GmbH, includes OpenStreetMap
    contributors (ODbL) and NASA / METI data. https://maps.eox.at/
  Satellite: EOxCloudless 2025, modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2025.
    https://cloudless.eox.at/
  Night: Black Marble composite, NASA Earth Observatory / EOX.
EOX terrain and Sentinel-2 cloudless 2018 layers are free for non-commercial
use only. Operators of other Strata instances must replace them
(see src/map-basemaps.ts).

Outlines: apps/web/public/geo/provinsi.geojson and kabupaten.geojson
Source: Badan Informasi Geospasial (BIG), Batas Wilayah administrative areas,
public map services at geoservices.big.go.id and kspservices.big.go.id.
Fetched 20 November 2026, simplified with mapshaper (Visvalingam, shapes kept),
provinces dissolved from kabupaten. Generalised for display; not for
delineating boundaries.

Place labels and dots are drawn from the story's own place metadata.
